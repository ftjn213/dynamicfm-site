隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

DynamicFM（以下称「本 App」）是一个收听全球网络电台的 App，由个人开发者 ZHEN ZHANG 开发。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本 App 处理哪些信息。

我们不收集你的个人信息

  • 本 App 不需要注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话等个人信息。
  • 本 App 不包含广告，不使用任何统计分析或追踪工具，也不会跨 App 追踪你。
  • 本 App 不请求定位、通讯录、照片、麦克风等权限。

保存在你设备上的数据

你收藏的电台保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传给我们。删除 App 时这些数据会一起删除。

本 App 会连接的第三方服务

为了提供电台播放功能，本 App 会直接连接以下服务。和访问任何网站一样，这些服务能看到你设备的 IP 地址等基本网络信息，并按各自的隐私政策处理：

  • Radio Browser（radio-browser.info）：社区维护的开放电台目录。本 App 从这里获取电台列表和搜索结果；你开始收听某个电台时，会按其建议向它上报一次该电台的「点击」，用于统计电台热度。上报内容只有电台编号，不包含任何能识别你身份的信息。
  • 各电台的播放服务器：收听时，音频直接从电台自己的服务器播放，电台图标也从电台提供的地址加载。
  • Apple 地图：App 中的卫星地球由 Apple 地图（MapKit）提供，适用 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有更新，我们会修改本页并更新上面的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

DynamicFM ("the App") lets you listen to internet radio stations from around the world. It is developed by independent developer ZHEN ZHANG. This policy explains what information the App handles.

We do not collect personal information

  • The App has no accounts or sign-in and does not collect your name, email, phone number or other personal information.
  • The App contains no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across apps.
  • The App does not request access to your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite stations are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are removed when you delete the App.

Third-party services the App connects to

To play radio, the App connects directly to the services below. As with visiting any website, they can see basic network information such as your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a community-maintained open station directory. The App loads station lists and search results from it. When you start playing a station, the App reports one "click" for that station, as Radio Browser recommends, so it can rank popular stations. The report contains only the station ID and nothing that identifies you.
  • Radio stations' streaming servers. Audio plays directly from each station's own server, and station logos load from addresses the stations provide.
  • Apple Maps. The satellite globe is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit) and is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
